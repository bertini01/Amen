# Privacy Policy — Amen

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Amen runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no telemetry. The developer receives no data about you or
how you use the app.

## What stays on your computer

Amen stores the following locally, in your Windows user profile, and never
uploads it anywhere:

- your settings;
- your download history (titles, source links, file locations);
- Audio Studio projects and the audio cache used to show waveforms;
- diagnostic logs, which you can copy yourself from the Diagnostics screen.

Uninstalling Amen removes this data.

## When Amen connects to the internet

Amen only goes online for the following:

- **Links you paste.** When you analyze or download a link, the built-in yt-dlp
  tool contacts that website to fetch the media, the same way your browser would.
- **Installing or updating its tools.** If you choose to install or update
  yt-dlp or FFmpeg from the Diagnostics screen, Amen downloads them from their
  official sources (github.com for yt-dlp, gyan.dev for FFmpeg).
- **Update check.** At most once a day, Amen asks api.github.com for the latest
  yt-dlp version number so it can tell you when an update is available.
- **Connection check.** The Diagnostics screen tests whether a well-known site
  (github.com, gstatic.com or youtube.com) can be reached. It opens a connection
  only and sends no data.

These websites may log basic connection information such as your IP address,
as with any web request. Their own privacy policies apply.

## Cookies file (optional)

You can choose to give Amen a cookies file in Settings → Advanced to reach
media you are signed in to. Amen only reads a file you select yourself. It
never reads cookies from your browsers and never sends the file anywhere
except to the website you are downloading from.

## Children

Amen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
